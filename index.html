About

I'm an Android developer based in Ahmedabad who turns ideas into scalable, reliable and easy-to-use mobile apps.

My core stack is Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and MVVM. I focus on production-ready apps that stay maintainable as they grow, with clean architecture, solid performance and third-party integrations that simply work.

I've built apps across freight and logistics, fintech and crypto, healthcare, marketplaces and commerce, music and media streaming, social video, accessibility and on-demand services, from complex features and SDK integrations to performance tuning and the final polish before launch.

Outside work, I design and publish my own indie games on Google Play. It's where I experiment freely and own the whole journey, from concept and code to launch and user feedback.
